SynthOslek — Controls & MIDI

    
Performance

    

      
Scenes A–DSelect a slot, then Save Scene to store all 4 layers + mix. Click the letter again to recall.

      
MIDI: ALL / CHALL = every layer plays. CH = MIDI channel 1–4 maps to layers 1–4.

      
Pitch / Mod wheelsPitch ±200¢ (springs to center). Mod drives LFO depth.

      
Gliss / Pitch / ScrollGliss = slide notes. Pitch = bend + vertical param (↕). Scroll = drag up/down or toward top/bottom of keys to shift octaves (GarageBand-style).

      
Voice limitMax 24 simultaneous voices; oldest notes are stolen when full.

    

    
MIDI Continuous Controllers

    

      
CC 1Mod wheel → LFO depth

      
CC 7Volume

      
CC 10Pan (active layer)

      
CC 64Sustain pedal

      
CC 20–23Layer 1–4 volume (SMK faders)

      
CC 28–31Layer 1–4 pan

      
CC 36–39Layer 1–4 FX send

      
CC 44–47Layer 1–4 enable (≥64 = on)

      
Pitch bend±200 cents (shown on pitch wheel)

    

    
Tips

Text SoundDescribe a sound or tap a chip, then ✨ MAKE SOUND
